# Product blueprint: "school in a box" (Nursery to Primary 5, Nigeria)

## What is being sold
1. The platform (this repo): white-label school management software, one subdomain per school.
2. The playbook: everything a founder needs to run a physical school, packaged as templates, a curriculum pack, and training. Recurring revenue comes from software subscriptions, licensing, and training and support.

## School structure
- Early years: Pre-Nursery/Creche (optional), Nursery 1 and 2. Lower primary: Primary 1 to 3. Upper primary: Primary 4 and 5.
- Three terms a year, about 12 to 13 teaching weeks each. Class size about 10 to 15 for nursery, 20 to 25 for primary.
- Note: ending at Primary 5 means pupils leave before finishing primary. Plan to add Primary 6 later or position as a feeder school.
- Curriculum: nursery is play-based (phonics, number work, rhymes, motor skills, a Nigerian language). Primary: English, Mathematics, Basic Science, Social Studies, Civic Education, Verbal and Quantitative Reasoning, Computer Studies, Creative Arts, PE, Religious Studies, a Nigerian language.
- Compliance: CAC registration plus State Ministry of Education approval and inspection. Requirements vary by state; verify before packaging.

## Seven areas every school needs
Academic core; People (staff roles, hiring, safeguarding); Operations (admissions, fees, attendance, communication); Legal and compliance; Facilities and tech; Business model (pricing, break-even at 30/60/100 pupils); Quality control (KPIs, audits).
Physical-school additions: site checklist, building and safety codes, power and water backup, transport, meals, maintenance, emergency drills, startup budget, month-by-month launch timeline.

## Student registration and onboarding (8 stages)
Enquiry; Application; Assessment (nursery: informal observation; primary: English and Maths placement plus parent interview); Admission decision (offer, waitlist, decline); Acceptance and enrolment (signed agreement, fees); Documents (birth certificate or age declaration, photos, immunisation record, previous report or transfer letter, emergency contacts, authorised pick-up persons); Onboarding (orientation, handbook, uniform and books, class placement, nursery settling-in plan, parent channel); First 30 days (check-ins at day 3, week 2, week 4).
Templates to produce: enquiry log, application form, assessment sheets, offer/waitlist/decline letters, acceptance form, parent-school agreement, medical and emergency form, pick-up authorisation, student file checklist, orientation checklist, welcome pack, 30-day feedback form.

## Sellable tiers
Base: paper playbook and templates. Mid: curriculum and scheme-of-work pack. Premium: the software portal, white-label, with training and ongoing support.
